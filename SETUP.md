## GitHub Pages

Upload the following to your public repository:
- index.html
- booking.html
- admin.html
- style.css
- script.js
- assets/yardscape-logo.jpg

Then GitHub: Settings -> Pages -> Deploy from branch -> main -> / (root).

## Admin

Open your live site at /admin.html and enter the ADMIN_KEY from your Google Apps Script.
